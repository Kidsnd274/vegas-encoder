Tape Shelf

The library is a shelf of tape spines, colored by what the encode is for. Pull a tape out and its label and the editor open below it; the command, checks and size follow every change.

The memorable thing: the spine shelf. You find a preset by its color and its place, the sticker says what is inside, and a hatched corner warns that the selected FFmpeg can’t encode it before you start a render.

Contrast changes: muted text darkened from #6A706B (4.2:1 on the wall) to #575D58 (5.6:1 wall, 4.7:1 shelf). The editing green is darkened from #2E8B57 to #277A4C so white spine titles reach 5.3:1. Review amber carries ink text (6.8:1). Error #A42620 is 6.0:1 on the wall.
