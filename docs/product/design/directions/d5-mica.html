D5 Mica

The calm, native baseline: a Windows 11 app that looks like it shipped with the system, with a navigation pane, a Mica window, settings cards and expanders, and InfoBars for anything that needs attention.

The memorable thing: a live size and quality card at the top of the editor. It shows the estimated file size with an honest range, where the setting sits between a smaller file and higher quality, the video and audio split, and what the choice means in plain words.
