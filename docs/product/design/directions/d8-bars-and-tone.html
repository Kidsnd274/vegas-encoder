Bars and Tone

Direction 8 for Vegas Encoder. Broadcast identity as status.

Idea. Colour bars and a reference tone are how broadcast checks that video and audio arrive intact. Here the bar colours become the editor’s sections: video is cyan, audio green, container magenta and the command yellow. Every section carries a thin edge in its colour, and the command preview uses the same colours for the arguments each section produces.

Memorable thing. The bar strip under the title bar is live section status. Each bar names its section and current setting; click one to jump there. Red stays dark until something would stop a render, blue is the FFmpeg in use, and white is what VEGAS delivers.

      Contrast: the brief’s muted grey #9A9DA3 measures 4.34:1 on raised panels, so it is lifted to #A4A7AD (4.89:1). Bar fills keep the brief’s values; red, magenta, green and blue text uses lighter tints (error #F58C82, 5.0:1 on raised). Mockup only: nothing here ran in VEGAS.
