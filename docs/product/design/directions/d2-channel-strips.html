Channel Strips

Idea. Streams are channels. Video and audio are vertical console strips, the container is the master strip, and presets are scene-recall buttons across the top. The metaphor is structure and colour only.

Memorable thing. The vertical quality fader: its scale says what the number means for the chosen encoder, so CRF 18 reads as “Visually lossless”, and higher quality is always up.

Contrast changes from the brief: muted text #5D6872 measured 4.41:1 on the faceplate, so it is #56616B (4.90:1). White text on the master cap #D8443A is 4.37:1, so the cap plate is #C93D33 (5.00:1) with the brief colour as its top band. Ok green #2F9E58 is used for marks only; ok text is #1D6B3C. Errors use #B3261E (5.06:1 on the faceplate).
