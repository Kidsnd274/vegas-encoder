Vegas Encoder design round 1, direction D3

Signal Flow

See the path from the VEGAS timeline to the file as a fixed diagram. VEGAS feeds the video and audio encoders, they join in the container, and the container writes the file. Selecting a node opens its settings below, next to the command.

The memorable thing: wires that thin where precision is lost. Wire thickness is bit depth, and an amber pip marks every pixel-format conversion, so “10-bit becomes 8-bit here” is visible at a glance.
