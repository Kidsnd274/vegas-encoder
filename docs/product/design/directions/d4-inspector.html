D4 Inspector

A dense, dark, keyboard-first panel that sits comfortably next to VEGAS: a library tree, a property grid for every setting, and an output pane that keeps the command, checks and size in view.

The memorable thing: one search box filters every setting at once, from curated fields to every option FFmpeg reports for the encoder. Anything changed from its default carries a gold bar in the grid, and the same gold in the command.
