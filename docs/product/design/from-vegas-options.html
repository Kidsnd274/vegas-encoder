What Vegas Encoder actually knows before render starts

Until a render begins in VEGAS, Vegas Encoder does not know the project’s frame size, frame rate, audio sample rate, channel count, or timeline length. What it does know is the pipe transport format leaving VEGAS: uncompressed 10-bit 4:2:0 (P010) video frames and 32-bit float PCM audio samples. Those pipe formats are what drive the 10-bit / 32-bit float wires and conversion pips on the Signal Path diagram.
